# R1-010D legacy CLEAN pre-deployment safety plan

Status: production readiness blocked; no production database has been inspected or modified.

1. Before any deployment, an authorized operator must perform read-only inventory of every `inquiry_source_document` with `safety_state = 'CLEAN'`, including source ID, creation time, provenance and associated scan-job/audit metadata. Do not export document bytes, capability values or customer details into logs.
2. Trace each row to the scanner version, signature set, job, source digest, timestamps and audit history. Missing or ambiguous provenance is untrusted; the absence of a job or an old CLEAN flag does not imply complete scanning.
3. Until a separately approved remediation and download-access policy is applied, do not deploy or enable the download path where untrusted legacy CLEAN rows exist. On an isolated disposable database, reproduce that the existing terminal CLEAN state remains downloadable under the old policy; do not claim that the new finalize guard repairs it.
4. Review the inventory with the owner and security. If safe quarantine or access revocation requires changing terminal transitions or access behavior, prepare a versioned contract amendment and an audited, reversible migration plan for explicit approval. Never bulk-update production state under this task.
5. Only after approved remediation, validate that untrusted originals cannot be downloaded, retain originals and audit provenance, and confirm recovery and access with controlled synthetic fixtures. Functional re-enablement of CLEAN additionally requires a separately approved completeness policy and DB migration.

## Owner decision proposal: legacy CLEAN

**No production inventory has been run.** Approve a separately authorized read-only, least-privilege inventory before any rollout: count all terminal CLEAN records and inspect only document IDs, timestamps, job/source digest consistency, scanner and signature provenance, audit correlation and download eligibility. Missing, conflicting, or unprovable evidence is not automatically trustworthy. Produce aggregate results without originals, secrets or personal data; define a controlled process for reviewing sensitive metadata.

Options for owner/security review: (A) block all downloads for the affected deployment at the gateway/application boundary; (B) deny only unverified legacy documents through an approved, explicit provenance-aware download gate; (C) a versioned quarantine/reclassification migration with audited history. A is the simplest temporary containment; B needs a trustworthy, immutable provenance predicate and race-safe checks; C changes frozen terminal lifecycle and needs an amendment. None is authorized by this proposal. Do not silently delete or overwrite originals or infer trust from a CLEAN flag or source digest.

Any accepted approach needs a reviewed versioned contract amendment covering download visibility, provenance and state transitions as applicable, plus a transactional, additive or reversible migration and a rehearsed rollback that never re-enables unverified downloads. The rollback must preserve original bytes, source records and audit history; backup/restore validation and a reconciliation ledger are prerequisites. Test on disposable PostgreSQL: mixed legacy CLEAN with/without provenance, ordinary QUARANTINED/REJECTED/SCAN_ERROR, simultaneous download and policy activation, failure/rollback, repeated migration, replay and fencing, and authorized/unauthorized staff. No production migrations, read-only inventory or live policy change are authorized here.
