## Specification ambiguity and proposed resolution

1. `05_DOMAIN_MODEL.md` draws a linear NEW → TRIAGE → NEEDS_CLARIFICATION → ENGINEERING_REVIEW path, while REQ-02 and the task express clarification and engineering review as branches. The proposed state machine permits TRIAGE to either branch and records clarification loops; OD-010-04 must approve these exact transitions.
2. Existing R1-002 FileObject/FileVersion has mandatory staff ownership and UNCHECKED-only versions; R1-006A is staff catalog import. Reusing those rows for anonymous Inquiry sources would violate their frozen ownership contract. The proposal reuses only the existing private StoragePort implementation and adds a separately typed InquirySourceDocument/intent boundary. No frozen table is modified.
3. `02_RELEASE_SCOPE.md` includes inquiry notifications and E2E-18 expects notification-failure resilience; `09_ROADMAP_BACKLOG.md` assigns delivery/retry to R1-017. R1-010B-01 therefore freezes no provider: Inquiry commit is independent of later after-commit notification delivery, and the E2E-18 delivery/retry acceptance remains R1-017.
4. R1-010A suggests 202, while the proposed endpoint transaction is synchronous. 201 is the recommended semantically exact response; OD-010-05 resolves owner preference.
5. REQ-03 and the domain sketch include a stage due date. The proposed contract exposes optional staff-managed `stageDueAt`, distinct from the customer's desired-deadline wording. It uses existing transition permission, OCC and audit; no SLA or notification automation is introduced.

## Final review corrections in this draft

- **OD-010-04 closure:** keep the single terminal CLOSED state. A strict `CloseInquiryWithoutQuoteRequest` requires one of DUPLICATE, CUSTOMER_WITHDRAWN, OUT_OF_SCOPE, SPAM or NOT_FEASIBLE and accepts only NEW, TRIAGE, NEEDS_CLARIFICATION, ENGINEERING_REVIEW or READY_FOR_QUOTE. A staff actor needs `inquiries.transition` plus current Inquiry read scope (`inquiries.read_all` for an unassigned NEW), If-Match and durable Idempotency-Key. Under the locked aggregate transaction, state/version, cleared `stageDueAt`, success AuditEvent and replay receipt commit together. A stale OCC result is durably replayed without mutation/success audit. Original submission data is immutable; CLOSED remains visible in authorized history and cannot reopen; no quote/order is created. QUOTE_SENT still requires an approved sent quote and can close only through quote lifecycle.
- **OD-010-03 practicality:** the proposed default MVP_SAFE accepts PDF/CSV/XLSX up to 10 MiB each, five files and 25 MiB per Inquiry, with a 100 MiB/source/UTC-day byte cap and 10 intent starts/source/hour. ENGINEERING_DOCUMENTS is an explicit server-only opt-in at 50 MiB/file, 20 files, 250 MiB/Inquiry, 1 GiB/source/day and 40 intent starts/source/hour. Shared NAT/VPN colleagues share source limits and can throttle one another. Both require backpressured StoragePort streams, 1 MiB app buffer/stream, four active streams/API process, two/source, 60-second idle timeout, a configured scanner before CLEAN/download, and generated exact-boundary/over-limit, concurrency, slow-stream, scanner and StoragePort fixtures. DOCX/DOCM/XLSM, DWG/DXF and archives remain rejected pending separate content validation, quarantine and safe-download strategy. PDF plots plus CSV/XLSX schedules can be submitted; without a real scanner staff cannot inspect the quarantined documents, so document-led cabinet work cannot proceed in R1-010. No scanner is assumed to exist.
- **PR #18 compatibility:** exact field comparison is in the table above. The client stays preview-only; its API submission and upload are not implemented. OD-010-01 remains stricter than current local contact validation. Future integration must require name plus email or phone, enforce the 50-line cap, preserve the product hint only as untrusted `catalogProductContext`, and implement capability/intent/stream upload.

These are contract questions, not modifications to decisions 1–108.
